The Story

Born from a box
of photographs.

Inside were images of an aunt who dressed like the world was her stage — pressed white suits, sweeping coats, gloves with everything. She moved through life with a style that didn't need a label to be unforgettable.

Salvaged Beauty is a love letter to her. To the women who dressed with intention before "vintage" was a category. To the pieces still out there, waiting to be found and worn again.

Every item in our collection has been chosen with her in mind.

Returns & Condition

Sold as-is, with care.

All of our items are vintage and pre-loved, so some natural wear and minor imperfections are to be expected. We take great care in handling each piece and do our best to clearly show and describe any flaws with detailed photos. Please note that we typically do not know the full history or prior storage conditions of each item, and slight variations in color or a faint vintage scent may be present.

We encourage you to reach out with any questions before purchasing—we're always happy to help! All items are sold as-is, and all sales are final.
